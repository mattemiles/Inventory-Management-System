QT / PYSIDE DESKTOP COMPONENTS

6.11.2, without modifications to those libraries. They are loaded dynamically
from the installation's _internal directory, rather than statically linked
into the application. The application's Python desktop source is provided in
the accompanying CMT source/build ZIP.
